What Teams adds

Recap stays free for individuals. Teams is in development. Early-access teams decide what gets built first.

  • A shared team library on your own server, so guides are one link away for everyone.
  • Automatic blurring of emails, names and account numbers in screenshots.
  • Company sign-in (SSO) and admin controls.
  • Managed installs for IT: Intune, MDM and silent install.
  • Your templates: guides that come out in your company's format.

Planned price: $10 per person a month, only for people who make guides. Everyone who reads them is free. Early-access teams get founding pricing.
