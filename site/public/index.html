Screen + a camera bubble you can drag anywhere. Mac, Windows, Linux, or the browser. Global hotkey to start and stop.

Word-level transcript and captions from whisper.cpp, running locally. Click any word to jump there.

Remove filler words and shorten long pauses. See the time saved, preview, then export the clean cut.
